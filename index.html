How it works. The model's forecasts were generated offline in Python for . For each product-store pair the page stores two numbers: average forecast demand per day and the standard deviation of the forecast error. Everything else is computed live with the same formulas as the notebook: lead-time demand = daily demand × lead time; safety stock = z × error std × √lead time; reorder point = lead-time demand + safety stock; reorder when inventory + on-order ≤ reorder point; order quantity = Math.ceil(Math.max(lead_time_demand, 1)). Lead time defaults to 7 days, the value used in the notebook, and is assumed fixed, with daily forecast errors independent. Service levels use z = 1.2816 (90%), 1.645 (95%) and 2.0537 (98%). This is a sample of 150 product-store pairs (15 products × 10 stores), not the full catalog.
